- Project-generated AI images, original prompt phrases, generated Flite clips, and sanitized email condensations are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- The real image files retain the public-domain or CC0 status and attribution recorded per item in `data/dataset-manifest.json`.
- SpeechBrain test audio is included under Apache License 2.0 and converted to Opus without changing its classification.
- The source email package declares its database under PDDL 1.0 and its contents under CC0 1.0. All included messages have identifying information, active links, markup, and unsafe content removed.
